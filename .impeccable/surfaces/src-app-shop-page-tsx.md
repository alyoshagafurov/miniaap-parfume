# Storefront — catalog surfaces

Scope: home, category, product, menu, request (cart). Mode: Operate — a wholesale buyer on a phone inside Telegram finds a fragrance in a format and adds it to a request.

Pinned by the client: their fashion-app reference, "as close as possible", same harmonised colours, large rounded blocks, name and price prominent, description and secondary facts small below. Confirmed: two ambers with one job each; dark blocks inside light screens, no dark screen; graphite and the logo's gold replace olive, which survives only in the ÁRUMI wordmark.
